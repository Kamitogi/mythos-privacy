Mythos Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Mythos ("the app," "we," "us") is a tabletop RPG campaign companion app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account information — such as your email address and display name, used to create and secure your account.
  • Campaign and character content — character sheets, campaign details, notes, and other content you create or upload within the app.
  • Chat messages — in-character, out-of-character, and GM messages sent within your campaigns.
  • Uploaded images — maps, character art, and other images you add to a campaign.
  • Push notification token — a device identifier used to deliver notifications to your device, if you enable them.

How we use your information

We use the information above to operate and improve the app: to let you create and play in campaigns, sync content between the players in your group, send push notifications you've opted into, and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

Data retention and deletion

We retain your account and campaign data for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us using the information below.

Children's privacy

Mythos is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
