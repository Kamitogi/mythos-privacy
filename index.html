Mythos Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mythos ("the app," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the app. By using Mythos, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information — such as your email address and display name, used to create and secure your account.
  • Campaign and character content — character sheets, campaign details, notes, and other content you create or upload within the app.
  • Chat messages — in-character, out-of-character, and GM messages sent within your campaigns.
  • Uploaded images — maps, character art, and other images you add to a campaign.
  • Push notification token — a device identifier used to deliver notifications to your device, if you enable them.

We do not knowingly collect precise location, contacts, SMS/call data, financial information, or health data, and the app does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs.

How we use your information

We use the information above to operate and improve the app: to let you create and play in campaigns, sync content between the players in your group, send push notifications you've opted into, moderate reported content, and maintain the security and reliability of the service. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

How we share your information

We do not sell your data. We share information only with the service providers that help us run the app, and only to the extent needed for them to provide that service:

ServiceWhat it's used forWhat it may receive
SupabaseDatabase, authentication, and file storageAccount info, campaign/character content, chat messages, uploaded images
ExpoApp build and over-the-air update infrastructureApp version and device/platform information needed to deliver updates
Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)Delivery of push notificationsPush notification token only

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms, and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Mythos, our users, or the public.

Data security

Data sent between the app and our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to the underlying database is restricted using row-level security so that, in general, players and GMs can only read and write data for campaigns they belong to. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or review your info — you can view and update your account and profile information at any time from within the app.
  • Push notifications — you can turn campaign and group notifications on or off individually from the app's Settings, or disable notifications entirely from your device's operating system settings.
  • Delete your account — you can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from within the app (Settings → Edit Profile → Delete Account). You can also request deletion without using the app by emailing us at LostSkiesVG@gmail.com.
  • Access, correction, portability, and objection — depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal data, such as the right to request a copy of it, correct it, or object to certain uses. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

Data retention and deletion

We retain your account and campaign data for as long as your account remains active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data and associated content within a reasonable time, except where we are permitted or required to retain limited information — for example, to prevent fraud or abuse, to resolve disputes, or to comply with a legal obligation.

International data transfers

Our service providers (Supabase, Expo, and Firebase Cloud Messaging) may store and process data on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. By using the app, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in these locations.

Children's privacy

Mythos is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Significant changes may also be communicated within the app.

Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, your data, or want to exercise any of the rights described above, contact us at LostSkiesVG@gmail.com.
